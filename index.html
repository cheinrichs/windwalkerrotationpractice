Keybinds

Click a key, then press the key or mouse button you use in game. Two abilities can share a key. Modifier combos (Shift, Ctrl, Alt), numpad keys and mouse buttons 3–5 all work. Saved in this browser.

Ability icons

Upload an image for any ability and it shows on its button. Square images look best. Icons are saved in this browser only.

The priority comes from Method's Windwalker playstyle and rotation guide (Patch 12.1, Conduit of the Celestials). The trainer assumes Ascension (6 Chi max), no Obsidian Spiral or Shadowboxing Treads, and that you never repeat the ability you just used (Combo Strikes). Chi costs are approximate: Tiger Palm +2, Blackout Kick 1, Rising Sun Kick 2, Spinning Crane Kick 2, Strike of the Windlord 2, Fists of Fury 3, Zenith Stomp +2. Rushing Wind Kick replaces the Rising Sun Kick button while its proc is up. Combo Breaker makes Blackout Kick free, and Dance of Chi-Ji makes Spinning Crane Kick free.
